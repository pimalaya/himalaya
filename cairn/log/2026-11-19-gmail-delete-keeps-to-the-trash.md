# Gmail delete keeps to the trash

The owner ruled on 2026-11-19 to keep Himalaya's Gmail delete as it is: permanent (`users.messages.delete`) only from `TRASH`, a move to `TRASH` from any other label, as neverest deletes for good from `TRASH` alone. Unlabelling stays `message move` or `gmail messages modify --remove-label`; a unified unlabelling verb would be a new verb, not planned. The change closes as abandoned, nothing landed.

## Capabilities moved

None: the spec already states the behaviour.
