# Wide lists print as records

The owner's design of 2026-11-11: a list whose items carry more than one value of unbounded length prints as pimalaya-cli's `records::Records`, the way `git log` prints commits; a list whose cells but the last are short stays `Columns`. pimalaya-cli comes from the ../cli checkout through a `[patch.crates-io]` path until `records` is released.

- Envelopes (`envelope list|search`): the id, then `unread`, `answered`, `flagged` and `attachment` in parentheses, each only when it applies (`attachment` only on `hasAttachment: true`, whether or not `--has-attachment` was passed, so a backend that always knows, Maildir among them, always says it); `From:` or `To:` as full `Name <email>` addresses, `Date:` as configured, `Size:`; the subject as the body, none when empty. `UNSEEN_GLYPH`, `REPLIED_GLYPH`, `FLAGGED_GLYPH`, `ATTACHMENT_GLYPH`, `format_flags` and `format_attachment` are gone for `format_state`; `EnvelopeListOutput.with_attachment` is gone.
- The same shape for `jmap email query|get` (gaining `answered` from `$answered`), `msgraph messages list` (gaining `flagged` and `attachment`, `U` becoming `unread`), `pimdir search` and `pimdir thread` (through `PimdirListStyle::hits`, gaining the envelope's words; subject then snippet as the body; `Matched:` only when a text term matched), `pimdir search --threads` (`-` titling a message no thread holds), `pimdir tag` (its status as the word, `Row:` only when staged), the queued messages of `pimdir queue list` (`Action:`, `Queued:`, `To:`; the parked rows stay columns), `attachment list|download` (`inline` as the word, `Path:` once written; `with_inline` and `with_path` gone), `pimdir attachment list|search` (`From:` now `Name <email>`), `msgraph attachments list`, `msgraph mail-folders list|get|child-folders`, `gmail settings filters list` (`Criteria:` and `Action:` as `get` prints them) and `gmail history list`, which lists the message ids of each record where it printed counts.
- The `Incomplete:` coverage line (`PimdirMailCoverage::write_under`), the pimdir attachment `Next page:` and the queue note print a blank line under the records. The `Next page:` of `Paginated` (pimalaya-cli), under `envelope list|search`, `msgraph messages list` and `gmail history list`, does not yet.
- `format_addresses` prints `Name <email>`, so `imap fetch` prints its addresses whole again, as v2.2.1 did; the names-only form was an unreleased regression of plain-text-output.
- Kept as columns on audit: `account list`, `account capability`, `mailbox list`, `imap list`, `imap search|sort`, the flag lists, `sieve list`, `jmap identity get`, `jmap mailbox query|get`, `jmap submission query`, `jmap thread get`, the Gmail labels, drafts, messages and threads lists, `gmail settings send-as|delegates|forwarding-addresses list`, `maildir|m2dir|mbox list`, `pimdir mailbox list` and `pimdir flag list`.
- Capabilities moved: commands (Text output is plain text), config (No colour is configured, Envelope dates MAY render relative to today), backends (The envelope carries its threading pointers, The pimdir subcommand reads and retracts the queue).

Verified: clippy over the 15 feature sets, the whole suite with all features, the build without SMTP; a byte-exact envelope list test, and new ones for the attachment list and a pimdir search hit above its coverage line; tests/repl.rs reads the record. The `json-schema` output of the previous build and this one are identical (223 files), and `--json` of `envelope list|search` and `attachment list` match on a scratch Maildir. The MCP `tools/list` differs only in the `has-attachment` description of `envelope_list` and `envelope_search`, which named the ATT column.
